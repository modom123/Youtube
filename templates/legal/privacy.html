Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026 | Last Updated: June 23, 2026

Social Money, Inc. ("Company", "we", "our", or "us") operates the Social Money platform (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, password, and profile information (creator name, niche, audience, goals). This information is required to provide the Service.

Video Content & Metadata

We collect and store:

  • Videos you create using our platform
  • Scripts, voiceovers, and raw video materials
  • Metadata about your content (topics, themes, performance data)
  • Publishing history and scheduling data

Platform Connection Data

When you authorize us to publish to social platforms, we receive and store:

  • OAuth tokens for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, and Threads
  • Platform account identifiers
  • Permissions to post on your behalf

Analytics & Engagement Data

We automatically collect:

  • Page views and feature usage
  • Video creation and publishing metrics
  • Engagement data from platforms you connect
  • IP address and browser information
  • Cookies and local storage

Payment Information

We do not directly handle credit card data. Stripe, our payment processor, collects and manages all billing information. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for:

  • Service Provision — Creating, editing, and publishing your videos
  • Platform Communication — Publishing your content to social networks on your behalf
  • Analytics & Improvement — Understanding how you use the Service and improving features
  • Research & Development — Improving our AI models (with opt-out available)
  • Customer Support — Responding to your inquiries and technical issues
  • Legal Compliance — Complying with law enforcement requests and regulatory obligations
  • Marketing — Sending newsletters and updates (you can opt-out anytime)

3. AI Training & Opt-Out

Some of your data may be used to improve our AI models, including script quality, video generation, and trend analysis. You can opt out of AI training in Settings → Privacy → Disable AI Training. When disabled, your content will not be used to train our models.

4. Data Storage & Security

Where We Store Your Data:

  • Application databases hosted on Render
  • Video files stored in encrypted S3-compatible storage
  • Backups encrypted and stored geographically

Security Measures:

  • All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3)
  • Sensitive data encrypted at rest (AES-256)
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Role-based access control (RBAC)
  • Rate limiting and DDoS protection

5. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you're in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Access — Request all data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification — Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure — Request deletion (with exceptions)
  • Right to Portability — Download your data in standard format
  • Right to Object — Opt-out of processing

To exercise these rights, email privacy@socialoptimize.online.

6. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

If you're in California, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to Know — What personal information is collected and used
  • Right to Delete — Request erasure of your data
  • Right to Opt-Out — Opt-out of sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — Exercising these rights won't result in discrimination

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Stripe — Payment processing (Privacy Policy)
  • Google OAuth — Authentication and YouTube API access (Privacy Policy)
  • TikTok, Instagram, Facebook OAuth — Platform authorization (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook)
  • SendGrid — Email delivery
  • Sentry — Error tracking and monitoring
  • Anthropic — AI model providers for content generation

8. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for:

  • Session Management — Keeping you logged in
  • Analytics — Understanding feature usage (Google Analytics)
  • Preferences — Remembering your settings

Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function properly.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Account Data — Retained until you delete your account
  • Video Content — Retained for 180 days after deletion (backup purposes); then permanently deleted
  • Analytics Data — Retained for 24 months, then aggregated
  • Log Data — Retained for 90 days for security purposes
