Our Story

Social Optimize was born from a simple observation: small businesses spend thousands of dollars on video production but never see the ROI. Content creators spend hours filming, editing, and uploading only to get lost in the noise.

We started by asking a different question: What if AI could handle the mechanical parts of content creation? Not to replace creativity, but to eliminate the busywork. What if a creator could spend their time on strategy and storytelling while AI handled research, scripting, voiceover, video generation, and publishing?

In 2023, we assembled a team of AI researchers, filmmakers, and product designers with a single goal: build the most capable content creation platform on earth. Today, Social Optimize powers creators and teams across 150+ countries, producing over 10,000 videos per week.

Our Mission

To democratize video production and put professional content creation tools in the hands of anyone with a story to tell. No expensive equipment. No editing skills required. Just ideas.
